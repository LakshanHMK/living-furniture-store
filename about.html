Our Story

Designed for Mindful, Everyday Living

Founded with a conviction that furniture should bring calm rather than clutter, Luma Living creates quiet architectural designs that respect both the space they inhabit and the forests they originate from.

We partner directly with master woodworkers across Northern Europe to produce heirloom-grade pieces from responsibly harvested solid oaks, walnuts, and breathable natural textiles. No shortcuts, no fast furniture.

Our Principles

What Drives Our Work

Three core commitments that guide each blueprint, cut of timber, and cushion we craft.

Enduring Craft

Traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery paired with modern precision engineering ensures that every piece lasts generations.

100% Sustainable Woods

We harvest timber solely from certified replanted forests, utilizing eco-conscious wax finishes with zero toxic fumes.

Honest Pricing

By designing in-house and delivering straight from European ateliers, we cut out showroom middlemen markups.
